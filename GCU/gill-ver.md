GCU Version Status

Current GCU version: "v1.pre-dev.0"

GCU is currently in the pre-development stage. Its utilities and components are being established and organized as part of the Gill/SeL4 environment.

"v1.pre-dev.0" does not represent a complete GCU release. It represents the current development state before the first complete GCU v1 release.

GCU v1.0.0

"GCU v1.0.0" will be reached when the required GCU files and core utilities have been implemented and made available as a complete GCU set.

The version progression is therefore independent from Gill's operating-system version:

Gill
└── v0.alpha.0.nt.nfs

GCU
└── v1.pre-dev.0
    └── v1.0.0  → complete initial GCU set

GCU is developed as its own component within Gill/SeL4, while remaining aligned with Gill's principles of minimalism, modularity, independence, and simple interfaces.
